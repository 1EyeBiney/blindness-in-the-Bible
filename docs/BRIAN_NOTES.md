# Brian's own words

Answers Brian gives to questions asked along the way, kept verbatim so the
personal sections of the site are drafted from what he actually said.
Lightly corrected for typing only.

## 2 October 2026

**Asked:** When did "we walk by faith, not by sight" first become your
verse, and what was happening in your life at the time?

**Brian:** I have slowly come to having this as my verse because it took a
while to learn to walk by faith. This is true for those transitioning to
non-visual means in life as well. It takes some time to learn to trust new
methods and tools, like a white cane or a screen reader.

**Earlier the same day, on why he wants this project:** I am blind and a
Christian. I do think that God has a deep care for the blind. I truly
believe that we "walk by faith and not by sight", so that saying has a lot
of meaning for me.
