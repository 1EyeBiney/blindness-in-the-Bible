# Not By Sight: concept

Written 2 October 2026 from Brian's answers. This is the agreed starting
point. Nothing here is built yet. Change this file first when a decision
changes.

## What it is

A series of studies on blindness in the Bible, written by a blind
Christian. "We walk by faith, not by sight" (2 Corinthians 5:7) is the
heart of it. Brian's conviction going in: God has a deep care for the
blind.

## Where it lives

- Repository: `blindness-in-the-Bible` (the local folder name, Brian's
  choice). Address: https://1eyebiney.github.io/blindness-in-the-Bible/

- Brian's portal for most of his work is to be notbysite.com, which he
  thinks of as NBS.
- This study is, in his mind, the "Not By Sight" site. The name
  notbysight.com is not available, so it will probably only ever have a
  GitHub address.
- It is a separate site for now, reached from the portal, and can be folded
  in later.
- Brian will test on the live address, not only on his own machine.

## Who it is for

Blind believers, their families, and pastors and churches. All three.

## Shape of each study

1. What the text says. The reading, with the passage quoted.
2. What it means for a blind reader. Brian's voice, from experience.
3. What it asks of the church. Real examples from the Nigeria work and
   First Aid for the Blind where they fit.

## Proposed order of work

1. The catalog. Sort all 86 verses into physical, legal and figurative.
   Brian reviews the sorting.
2. The people. Every blind person in Scripture: who was healed, who was
   not, what the text says of each.
3. The healings. What the accounts share and where they differ.
4. The law. How God commands His people to treat the blind.

## Cautions

- A word search misses passages that describe blindness without the word
  (Isaac's eyes "too dim to see", Eli, Ahijah). The catalog needs a second
  pass.
- Some verses use the word without being about blind people
  ("blindfolded", a bribe that "blinds" the wise).
- The files are English only. Hebrew and Greek word studies need a
  reliable source, not memory.
- The Berean text is public domain (dedicated 30 April 2023; terms read on
  berean.bible, 2 October 2026). The publisher asks that copies which differ
  from the official text not carry the Berean name. Brian's decision,
  2 October 2026: use the publisher's official text, word for word, and
  attribute it. It is kept at data/raw/berean/bsb.txt.

## Working rules carried over from Gridiron Greatness

Plan first. Everything reproducible from the source text. Prose checked
against the text like code is checked by tests. State the limits. Ask
before publishing. Screen reader first: this audience makes accessibility
the top requirement, not a feature.
