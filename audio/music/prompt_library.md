# Not By Sight: Suno prompt library

A palette, not a score. Eight style families for the series, each a prompt in the same grammar as the Tentmakers
beds so everything stays one sound: oud, lyre, reed, frame drum, low drone; no vocals, no choir, no modern drums,
no cinematic bombast; background for speech. Run each prompt several times in Suno and keep the versions you like
(v1, v2, ...). Choose by ear in Reaper. The settings named below are only to say what each family is for; use any
family anywhere the dialogue wants it.

Capture through Reaper loopback at 44.1 kHz, 24-bit. Name files "<family number> <family name>_v<n>.mp3" so the
credit line can be matched later.

---

## 1. Roadside morning (open air, still)

For: edges of towns, a man sitting where he sits every day, openings and closings outdoors.

Prompt: Ancient Near Eastern open-air ambient, dry desert valley morning, soft oud, sparse lyre, warm reed drone,
very light frame drum like a slow walk, wide open sky, dusty roadside outside a palm town, heat rising, unhurried
and grounded, close-miked with no hall reverb, no vocals, no choir, no modern drums, no cinematic bombast,
background score for spoken narration.

## 2. Pilgrim road (open air, walking pulse)

For: crowds on the move, journeys, the road up to Jerusalem, following a teacher through streets.

Prompt: Ancient caravan road ambient, steady walking frame drum pulse like many sandals on packed earth, oud and
lyre in a travelling rhythm, reed melody drifting in and out as if from a passing group, warm valley air, palms
and groves, open space with no echo, pilgrims on the road to a feast, purposeful and warm, no vocals, no choir, no
modern drums, background score for spoken narration.

## 3. Lakeshore dawn (open air, water)

For: Bethsaida, Capernaum, boats, the Sea of Galilee, anything by water in the early light.

Prompt: Ancient lakeshore ambient at dawn, soft oud, gentle lyre, low reed drone like mist on still water, very
slow frame drum like oars, fishing village waking, nets and boats, cool air with a faint shimmer, open space with
no hall reverb, calm and expectant, no vocals, no choir, no modern drums, background score for spoken narration.

## 4. Hill country (open air, pastoral)

For: villages, mountainsides, sheep and goats, people climbing with someone on their arm, the crowds on the
mountain.

Prompt: Ancient hill-country pastoral ambient, soft oud, light lyre, reed melody like a shepherd's pipe in the
distance, slow frame drum like a climb, dry grass and thyme, wind over rock, a crowd gathering on a mountainside,
warm and open with no echo, humble and hopeful, no vocals, no choir, no modern drums, background score for spoken
narration.

## 5. The crowd stops (open air, suspense into stillness, then warmth)

For: shouting over a crowd, tension, and the moment everything goes quiet; also healings at the turning point.

Prompt: Open-air crowd suspense into sudden stillness, ancient desert road, low bowed drone, sparse oud notes,
restrained frame drum like a crowd walking slowly, tension building as a shout goes unanswered, then the pulse
drops away into a held silence, then warm lyre and reed return as the crowd turns kind, dry air with no reverb, no
vocals, no choir, no modern percussion, leave space for shouted dialogue.

Note: Suno will not time the drop for you. Cut it in Reaper at the line where the crowd stops.

## 6. Daylight hope (open air, reflective)

For: the thinking sections, two stories set side by side, Zacchaeus's tree, "the Son of Man came to seek and to
save," any place the narration lifts without a climax.

Prompt: Desert hope ambient, open-air reflective underscore, soft oud harmonics, gentle lyre, faint reed melody,
low warm drone, barely-there frame drum, bright valley daylight, a sycamore fig by a road, measured hope with a
small lift and no climax, dry air with no reverb, no vocals, no choir, no modern drums, gentle background score for
spoken narration.

## 7. Temple courts (open stone, bright, busy)

For: Jerusalem's Temple Mount, porches and money changers, children shouting Hosanna, the blind and lame coming in.

Prompt: Ancient Temple courtyard ambient, bright open stone under full sun, oud and lyre with a lively but
restrained pulse, reed tone like a distant procession, soft frame drum like feet on paving, coins and cattle and
crowds in wide porches, grandeur without bombast, air and light rather than echo, no vocals, no choir, no modern
drums, background score for spoken narration.

## 8. Stone fortress (indoors, low, worn)

For: Machaerus and John in prison, dungeons cut in rock, doubt in a dark place; also the Pharisees' hall.

Prompt: Ancient stone fortress ambient, low bowed drone, sparse oud notes far apart, faint reed like wind through
a high slit window, very slow frame drum like a heartbeat, cold rock walls and a long drop outside, a prisoner
waiting for news, heavy but not hopeless, restrained close reverb of a small stone cell, no vocals, no choir, no
modern drums, no cinematic bombast, background score for spoken narration.

## Two words that steer the room

If a result comes back too churchy or too wet, add "close-miked, no hall" and try again. If it comes back too
bare, add "warm valley air" or "wind over rock." Keep the instrument list and the closing clause exactly the same
every time; that is what makes thirty tracks sound like one score.
