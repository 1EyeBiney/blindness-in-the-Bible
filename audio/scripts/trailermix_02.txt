[NARRATOR] (calm) This is Not By Sight. Stories of blind people in the Bible, made for listening, by a blind man who went looking for what Scripture says about people like him.

I know it by the slope under my feet, by the street narrowing, by the bread ovens on the left and the sound of a side street opening on the right. I have never been to the bottom.
