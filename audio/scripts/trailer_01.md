## 5. From Brian. NEW. Three short paragraphs.

BRIAN: I started this study to find out what the Bible says about blindness, now that I am on this side of the
curtain. I did not expect how much comfort I would find. I did not expect how often the blind are there, or how
much they mattered to the work of Jesus. Again and again, when a blind person is healed, it points everyone
around them to who He really is.
