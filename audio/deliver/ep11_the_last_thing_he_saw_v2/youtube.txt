Not By Sight, Episode 11: The Last Thing He Saw (2 Kings 25, Jeremiah 34 and 38, Ezekiel 12)

Zedekiah, the last king in Jerusalem, was made to watch his sons die and then blinded, so that it would be the last thing he ever saw. The episode opens with sound: what God made it to do, how it comes round corners and under doors, and the sounds a blind man cannot unhear. Then the king: a frightened man who kept sending for Jeremiah in secret and never acted on the answer; the night escape through the gate between the two walls; Riblah, told only as the text tells it; the road to Babylon; two prophecies that seemed to contradict each other; and the one promise he did not have to do anything to receive. Brian, blind since 2014, tells for the first time what the months of trying to save his eyes were like, and why what stays with him is what he heard.

This is episode 11 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the first of six on sight lost and not given back: six people in the Bible who lost their sight and lived the rest of their lives without it. Every episode begins with sounds. When a scene is imagined, we say so. Everything else is from the text, from the old books read in their own voices, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.
