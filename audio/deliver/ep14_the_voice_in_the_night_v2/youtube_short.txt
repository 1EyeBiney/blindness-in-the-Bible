Not By Sight, Episode 14: The voice in the night (1 Samuel 3 and 4)

The house of the LORD at Shiloh, in the middle of the night. The old priest in the bed cannot see, and a boy has just run to his bedside for the third time, sure that Eli called him. Eli did not. The episode opens there, with what a blind man hears in it: a very short list of the voices that can be in his house, and an old man who never heard the voice and still knew whose it was. Then where we are: Hannah weeping at the doorpost and Eli's wrong word and his blessing, the little robe a size bigger every year, the boy the old priest raised, the two sons he could not govern, and the man of God who told Eli that his own eyes would fail and his heart would grieve. First Samuel 3 read word for word: before the lamp of God had gone out, the LORD calls, and it is the blind man in the dark room who understands and tells the boy how to answer. In the morning he makes the boy tell him all of it, and hands it back: He is the LORD. Then the gate, years later, in one afternoon: an old man of ninety-eight on a chair beside the road, listening to it, a runner going past him into the town, the whole town crying out, and the one person in Shiloh who has to ask why. Two Hebrew words for Eli's eyes: the smouldering wick that is not put out, and eyes that have stood still. Brian, blind since 2014, on the patience you have whether you want it or not, on hearing what you cannot act on, on what changes when people decide you are totally blind, and on the sentence Eli taught a boy in the dark.

This is episode 14 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the fourth of six on sight lost and not given back: six people in the Bible who lost their sight and lived the rest of their lives without it. Every episode begins with sounds. When a scene is imagined, we say so. Everything else is from the text, from the old books read in their own voice, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.

The pictures, in words
The lamp at Shiloh from the end of episode 13. One small clay lamp burning low against a curtain and a stone wall. A boy seen from behind, running barefoot down a dark stone passage toward a lit doorway. An old man in a low bed, his head turned to the wall. Brian's picture. The episode map: Shiloh in gold, and the road west to Aphek. The open scroll. A boy sitting up on his mat beside the lamp, listening. A boy pushing open the tall doors at morning.
