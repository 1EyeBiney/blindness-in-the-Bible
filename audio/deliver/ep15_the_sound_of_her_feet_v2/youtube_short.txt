Not By Sight, Episode 15: The sound of her feet (1 Kings 14)

A small house in Shiloh, about a hundred and seventy years after Eli. The old man inside cannot see. Someone has just stopped at his door and has not said a word, and the first thing out of the house is her name. The episode opens there, with what a blind man hears at a door: Brian on why Ahijah did not recognize her footsteps, and what he had instead. Then where we are: the new cloak torn into twelve pieces on the road out of Jerusalem, the promise with a second half, the two golden calves, and the king who never once sent to the prophet until his boy fell ill. First Kings 14 read word for word: the disguise, the ten loaves and the jar of honey, the LORD telling the blind man who is coming before her feet reach the step. An imagined walk, a day from Tirzah, mostly uphill, and the half second in the doorway when she sees his eyes and knows the disguise was wasted on the one man she did it for. The word she carries home, and the first time anyone in the Bible says "beyond the Euphrates." The Hebrew word for Ahijah's eyes, the same word as Eli's at the gate: they stood. Brian, blind since 2014, on eyes that have stopped moving, on the family that could never tell who was in front of them, and on being less distracted in a world built to pull attention away from Jesus.

This is episode 15 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the fifth of six on sight lost and not given back: six people in the Bible who lost their sight and lived the rest of their lives without it. Every episode begins with sounds. When a scene is imagined, we say so. Everything else is from the text, from the old books read in their own voice, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.

The pictures, in words
The street at Shiloh crying out, from the end of the Eli episode, and the woman on the road from its close. A small stone house at the edge of a hill village, its doorway dark. Inside, a very old man on a bench facing the bright door, his face lost in shadow. A woman in a plain cloak seen from behind on the threshold, a basket on her arm. Brian's picture. A title card over the hill country at morning. A prophet in an open field tearing a bright cloak, the pieces on the grass, a younger man watching. The scroll. A crowd seen from behind on the road, a man on a mule at their head. A stepped stone shrine with a small golden calf on top. A child in a carved bed, his mother beside him, the king at the foot with his back to us. The episode map: Shiloh in gold, the road north to Tirzah. A lone woman with a basket on a road through green hills. The same woman in a village street, nobody looking twice. The basket close up, loaves and a clay jar under the cloth. Reeds bending in the wind at the edge of grey water. The Euphrates road from episode 11. Eli on his chair from episode 14. Three scenes on an old plaster wall: Isaac touching Jacob's arm, a dark tent with one lamp, brothers bowing before Joseph. Matthew Henry's study. A village from above, everyone on the move, one old man seated still in a doorway. Sandalled feet on a worn stone threshold. A torchlit hall, a boy leading a tall man by the hand toward two pillars.
