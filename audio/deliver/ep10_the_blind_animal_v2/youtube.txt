Not By Sight, Episode 10: The Blind Animal and the Altar (Leviticus 22, Deuteronomy 15, Malachi 1)

The last law in this series is not about blind people at all. It is about lambs: no animal that is blind, injured or maimed may be offered to the LORD. Brian, who has been blind since 2014, found it the hardest of the four to read, because it seems to put blindness in the category of broken. This episode answers that word with two Hebrew words, a market in the Temple, a prophet's test, and a look back over everything the Law said about the blind.

This is episode 10 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the last of four episodes on the Law: what God commanded about the blind. There are no imagined scenes in this series. Everything you hear is from the text, from the old books read in their own voices, from the Hebrew words, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.
