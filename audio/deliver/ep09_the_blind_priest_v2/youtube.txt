Not By Sight, Episode 9: The Blind Priest (Leviticus 21)

Of all the laws in the Bible that mention the blind, this is the only one that limits what a blind person may do: a priest who was blind could not approach the altar. Brian, who has been blind since 2014, did not argue with it. He asked three practical questions. What did a priest do with his hands? Would a blind priest touch what he should not? Would he have been a burden to the rest? This episode answers all three from the old books, and then asks what the law says about what a blind person is worth.

This is episode 9 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the third of four episodes on the Law: what God commanded about the blind. There are no imagined scenes in this series. Everything you hear is from the text, from the old books read in their own voices, from the Hebrew words, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.
