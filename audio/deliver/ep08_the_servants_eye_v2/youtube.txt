Not By Sight, Episode 8: The Servant's Eye (Exodus 21)

Two verses of law, and in them the only price the Bible ever puts on an eye. If a master blinds his servant, the servant goes free. If he knocks out a tooth, the servant goes free. Brian, who has been blind since 2014, read it and asked why an eye and a tooth should cost the same. The answer is in two Hebrew words, one for eye and one for free, and in what Babylon did with the same injuries.

This is episode 8 of Not By Sight, a study of blindness in the Bible made by a blind man, for listening. It is the second of four episodes on the Law: what God commanded about the blind. There are no imagined scenes in this series. Everything you hear is from the text, from the old books read in their own voices, from the Hebrew words, and from Brian. Scripture is read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible.
