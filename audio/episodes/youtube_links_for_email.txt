NOT BY SIGHT: THE EPISODES ON YOUTUBE

Not By Sight is a study of blindness in the Bible, made by a blind man, for listening. Each episode tells one story: the passage read word for word from the Berean Standard Bible, the place and the time, an imagined scene or two, and Brian's own thoughts, with music. Every video has captions, and the pictures are described in the notes under each video.

The full study, with every verse and source, is at https://blindnessinthebible.com


Episode 1: The Man Born Blind (John 9)
About 16 minutes.
Jesus makes mud, puts it on a blind beggar's eyes, and sends him to wash in the Pool of Siloam. The man comes back seeing, and then has to answer for it to his neighbours, his parents and the Pharisees.
https://youtu.be/y-fccElFytM


Episode 2: Bartimaeus at Jericho (Mark 10, Luke 18 to 19)
About 22 minutes.
A blind beggar at the roadside hears that Jesus of Nazareth is passing and will not be quieted. Jesus stops, the whole crowd stops with Him, and He asks, "What do you want Me to do for you?"
https://youtu.be/zzAgXrX9NEM


Episode 3: The Blind Man at Bethsaida (Mark 8)
About 18 minutes.
Jesus takes a blind man by the hand and leads him out of the village. This is the one healing in the Gospels that comes in two stages: first he sees people who look like trees, walking, and then he sees everything clearly.
https://youtu.be/M_LW64UXuxY


Episode 4: The Ones Who Followed (Matthew 9 and 12)
About 22 minutes.
Two blind men follow Jesus down the street, calling out, and keep following right into the house. Then a man who is both blind and mute is brought to Him by others, and the argument that follows forgets the man it was about.
https://youtu.be/kOQtHFpQvzs


Episode 5: The Blind in the Crowd (Matthew 15 and 21)
About 22 minutes.
Twice the blind are healed as part of a crowd, with no names given. Once on a mountain above the Sea of Galilee, where people carried each other up the hill and stayed three days. Once in the Temple at Jerusalem, in Jesus's last week.
https://youtu.be/lk5kVwj0D8k


For we walk by faith, not by sight. 2 Corinthians 5:7
