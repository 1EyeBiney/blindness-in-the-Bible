# Pickup 2 November 2026: Brian asked for two more takes of his own clip 025.
[BRIAN] {id=025} (calm) People ask me how I can trust anyone, living like this. The honest answer is that I have no choice, and neither did Isaac, and neither do you. You just do not notice, because your eyes let you pretend you checked. I check with everything I have, the way he did, and then I take the word of the person in the room. Most days that is enough. This chapter is the Bible's record of the day it was not, and the Bible does not blame the blind man for it. When the people he trusted failed him, Isaac trembled, and then he said, he shall be blessed. He had been trying to give the blessing to the son he favoured, against the word spoken to Rebekah. When he understood, he took the Lord's choice over his own. Sight is the sense that lets a person believe he walked by sight. Every blind man knows he walks by something else. This chapter is about which something else.
