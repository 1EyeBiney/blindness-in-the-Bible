# The series map, in words

Each map is drawn from real coordinates (audio/maps/places.json). The description below each one is the map for a listener who cannot see it. Distances are straight-line kilometres.

## base.png

Base map. The Great Sea lies to the west. The Jordan runs south from the Sea of Galilee to the Dead Sea. Jerusalem sits in the hills west of the Dead Sea's north end. Distances from Jerusalem:
- Jericho: 22 km to the north-east. Herod's Jericho stood a little south-west of the old mound; the two are drawn as one.
- Bethany: 3 km to the east. On the Jerusalem side of the Mount of Olives, the last stop on the road up from Jericho.
- Capernaum: 127 km to the north. Jesus's base in Galilee; Edersheim's placing of the two blind men, not certain.
- Bethsaida: 131 km to the north. Which ruin is Bethsaida is still argued; et-Tell is drawn, el-Araj lies 2 km south on the shore.
- Nazareth: 103 km to the north. Where Jesus read Isaiah 61, 'recovery of sight to the blind'.
- The mountain by the lake: 119 km to the north. Edersheim's placing, on the eastern shore in the Decapolis; Matthew gives only the Sea of Galilee and a mountain.
- Machaerus: 44 km to the south-east. Herod's fortress east of the Dead Sea, where John the Baptist was held and killed, by Josephus.
- Shiloh: 31 km to the north. Eli at the tabernacle; Ahijah's house, where Jeroboam's wife came in disguise.
- Gaza: 80 km to the west. Where Samson, blinded, ground grain and pulled down the temple of Dagon.
- Beersheba: 70 km to the south-west. Isaac's country; the blessing was given from his tent, place not named in Genesis 27.
- Samaria: 56 km to the north. Where Elisha led the blinded Syrian army and had their eyes opened.
- Dothan: 70 km to the north. Where the Syrians were struck blind and the servant's eyes were opened to the hills full of horses and chariots.
- Damascus: 217 km to the north-east. Saul, blinded on the road, was led in by the hand; Straight Street is still there.
- Caesarea Philippi: 169 km to the north. Just after Bethsaida in Mark, where Peter said 'You are the Christ'.
- Babylon and Riblah: far to the north-east, beyond the map. Zedekiah was blinded at Riblah in Syria and taken to Babylon; both lie beyond this map.
- Egypt: far to the south-west, beyond the map. Jacob blessed Joseph's sons in Egypt, beyond this map.
- Nineveh and Ecbatana: far to the north-east, beyond the map. Tobit's story, if included, lies in Assyria and Media, beyond this map.
