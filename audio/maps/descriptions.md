# The series map, in words

Each map is drawn from real coordinates (audio/maps/places.json). The description below each one is the map for a listener who cannot see it. Distances are given as walking time on the roads of the day, and directions as a clock face with north at twelve.

## base.png

Base map. The Great Sea lies to the west. The Jordan runs south from the Sea of Galilee to the Dead Sea. Jerusalem sits in the hills west of the Dead Sea's north end. Distances from Jerusalem:
- Jericho: about 7 hours' walk, at 2 o'clock from Jerusalem. Herod's Jericho stood a little south-west of the old mound; the two are drawn as one.
- Bethany: under an hour's walk, at 4 o'clock from Jerusalem. On the Jerusalem side of the Mount of Olives, the last stop on the road up from Jericho.
- Capernaum: about 3 and a half days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Jesus's base in Galilee; Edersheim's placing of the two blind men, not certain.
- Bethsaida: about 3 and a half days' walk, at 1 o'clock from Jerusalem. Which ruin is Bethsaida is still argued; et-Tell is drawn, el-Araj lies 2 km south on the shore.
- Nazareth: about 3 days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Where Jesus read Isaiah 61, 'recovery of sight to the blind'.
- The mountain by the lake: about 3 and a half days' walk, at 1 o'clock from Jerusalem. Edersheim's placing, on the eastern shore in the Decapolis; Matthew gives only the Sea of Galilee and a mountain.
- Machaerus: about a day's walk, at 4 o'clock from Jerusalem. Herod's fortress east of the Dead Sea, where John the Baptist was held and killed, by Josephus.
- Shiloh: about a day's walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Eli at the tabernacle; Ahijah's house, where Jeroboam's wife came in disguise.
- Gaza: about 2 days' walk, at 8 o'clock from Jerusalem. Where Samson, blinded, ground grain and pulled down the temple of Dagon.
- Beersheba: about 2 days' walk, at 7 o'clock from Jerusalem. Isaac's country; the blessing was given from his tent, place not named in Genesis 27.
- Samaria: about 1 and a half days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Where Elisha led the blinded Syrian army and had their eyes opened.
- Dothan: about 2 days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Where the Syrians were struck blind and the servant's eyes were opened to the hills full of horses and chariots.
- Damascus: about 6 days' walk, at 1 o'clock from Jerusalem. Saul, blinded on the road, was led in by the hand; Straight Street is still there.
- Caesarea Philippi: about 4 and a half days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Just after Bethsaida in Mark, where Peter said 'You are the Christ'.
- Shechem: about 1 and a half days' walk, at 12 o'clock from Jerusalem. Between Mount Gerizim to the south and Mount Ebal to the north, where the tribes stood to answer the blessings and curses (Deuteronomy 27).
- Babylon and Riblah: far to the north-east, beyond the map. Zedekiah was blinded at Riblah in Syria and taken to Babylon; both lie beyond this map.
- Egypt: far to the south-west, beyond the map. Jacob blessed Joseph's sons in Egypt, beyond this map.
- Nineveh and Ecbatana: far to the north-east, beyond the map. Tobit's story, if included, lies in Assyria and Media, beyond this map.
- Mount Sinai: far to the south, beyond the map. Where the Law was given, about eleven days' walk south of Jerusalem by the traditional site; beyond this map.
