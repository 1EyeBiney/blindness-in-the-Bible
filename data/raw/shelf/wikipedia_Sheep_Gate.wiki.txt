The '''Sheep Gate''' is a [[City gate|town gate]] in [[Trim, County Meath|Trim]], [[Republic of Ireland|Ireland]]. It is a [[National Monument (Ireland)|National Monument]].<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.geograph.ie/photo/2696393|title=Geograph:: Trim Town Wall: Sheep Gate (C) Anthony Foster|website=Geograph.ie}}</ref>

==History==
[[File:Wakeman Trim Yellow Steeple and Sheep Gate.png|thumb|Illustration from ''Wakeman's handbook of Irish antiquities'' (1903), showing Sheep Gate and [[Yellow Steeple]].]]
The Sheep Gate is the only surviving gate of five that once provided access to Trim. The [[Defensive wall|town wall]] and its gates were built in the 13th or 14th century. Sheep Gate may have been so named as a toll was charged here for [[sheep]] being brought in to be sold at market: in 1290, the [[murage]] and [[pavage]] tax was [[Penny (English coin)|one penny]] per ten sheep, reduced to a [[Farthing (English coin)|farthing]] in 1308. The name could also derive from the archaic meaning of [[wikt:cheap|cheap]], meaning "market" (cf. [[Cheapside]]). This name is not recorded before the 19th century; it may have been known as the Porch Gate, possibly from French ''porte'' ("door"), which may also give its name to the [[Porch Fields]] lying outside the city walls.<ref>{{Cite web|url=http://eprints.maynoothuniversity.ie/5121/2/Michael_Potterton_20140624130008.pdf|format=PDF|title=THE ARCHAEOLOGY AND HISTORY OF MEDIEVAL TRIM, COUNTY MEATH|author=Michael Potterton|website=Eprints.maynoothuniversity.ie|access-date=4 November 2021}}</ref> The gate was locked between 9 p.m. and 4 a.m.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.lonelyplanet.com/ireland/county-meath/trim/sights/landmarks-monuments/sheep-gate|title=Sheep Gate |website=Lonelyplanet.com}}</ref>

==The gate==
[[File:Trim - Sheep Gate and Castle.jpg|thumb|Sheep Gate with [[Trim Castle]] behind.]]
[[File:Sheeps Gate and Yellow Steeple at Trim Castle.jpg|thumb|left|Sheep Gate with Yellow Steeple behind.]]
[[File:Trim Castle - County Meath (3138577981).jpg|thumb|View of Sheep Gate, with Boyne in foreground.]]
The gate was the southeastern entrance to the town, and is located just north of the [[River Boyne]]. Sheep Gate survives as a stone archway.<ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.irishstones.org/place.aspx?p=403|title=Sheep Gate|website=Irishstones.org}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=http://www.trekearth.com/gallery/Europe/Ireland/North/Meath/photo1393186.htm|title=Ireland|website=Trekearth.com}}</ref>
